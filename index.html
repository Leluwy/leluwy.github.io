I have recently completed my M.Sc. in Data Science at ETH Zürich. During my graduate studies, I spent a semester as a visiting student in the Department of Computer Science & Engineering at the University of Washington.

Before that, I received my B.Sc. in Electrical Engineering and Information Technology from ETH Zürich.
